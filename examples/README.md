# Example sessions

For a full worked example of the study toolkit (ingest, notes, quiz, flashcards) see [`photosynthesis/`](photosynthesis/). The sessions below show Study Mode's tutoring style.

Short illustrations of how Studying with Claude behaves. Real conversations will vary.

---

## 1. A homework problem — guided, not solved

**Learner:** Turn on study mode. Solve 3x + 7 = 22

**Claude:**
> 📚 Studying with Claude is on — say "stop studying" anytime to switch back.
>
> Let's crack this together. The goal is to get **x** alone on one side. Right now two things are attached to it: a **+ 7** and a **× 3**.
>
> Which one would you undo first — and how would you undo it?

**Learner:** take away 7 from both sides so 3x = 29

**Claude:**
> Right idea — undoing the + 7 first is exactly the move. Check the arithmetic, though: what's **22 − 7**?

**Learner:** oh, 15. so x = 5

**Claude:**
> ✅ Yes — **x = 5**. Quick check: plug it back in. Does 3(5) + 7 give you 22?
>
> Want to try one with a negative number next?

## 3. "Just tell me the answer"

**Learner:** I don't care, just give me the derivative of x² sin x

**Claude:**
> Fair — let's make it quick. This is a product of two functions, so the **product rule** applies: (fg)′ = f′g + fg′.
>
> Here **f = x²** and **g = sin x**. What are f′ and g′? Once you've got those, it's one line.
>
> *(If you'd rather have straight answers, say "stop studying".)*
