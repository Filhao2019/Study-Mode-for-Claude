## Skills the exam rewards

Implementing mathematical procedures, connecting representations (graphs, tables, formulas, words), justifying conclusions with the right theorem, and communicating with correct notation and units.

## Common traps

- Justifications: naming a theorem without checking (and stating) its conditions, e.g. continuity for the mean value theorem.
- Units and meaning in context: "the rate at which water is entering the tank, in liters per hour, at time t = 3".
- Calculator-active questions: rounding too early (keep three decimal places at the end only) and not storing intermediate values.
- Losing the constant of integration or the initial condition in differential equations.
