# IB Diploma Programme: overview and core

> Starter outline, original summary. Official source: **International Baccalaureate** (ibo.org) and your school's IB coordinator, who has the subject guides and assessment calendar. Last checked: October 2026.

## Structure

- **Six subjects**, one from each group (or a second from groups 1–4 instead of group 6): 1 Studies in language and literature, 2 Language acquisition, 3 Individuals and societies, 4 Sciences, 5 Mathematics, 6 The arts.
- Usually **three at Higher Level (HL)** and three at Standard Level (SL); some students take four HL.
- **The core:** Theory of Knowledge (TOK), the Extended Essay (EE, an independent research essay of up to 4,000 words), and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS).

## Grading

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| Each of the six subjects | 1–7 |
| TOK and EE together (matrix of their A–E grades) | up to 3 |
| **Maximum** | **45** |

The diploma generally requires at least 24 points plus further conditions (completing CAS, no failing core grades, minimum HL and SL totals and others). Your coordinator has the current rules.

Each subject combines **external assessment** (final exams in May or November) with **internal assessment** (IA: coursework marked by teachers and moderated by the IB).

## Starter topics for a course (`id`)

- TOK: knowledge questions (`tok-knowledge-questions`), areas of knowledge (`tok-areas-of-knowledge`), the exhibition (`tok-exhibition`), the essay (`tok-essay`)
- Extended Essay: research question (`ee-research-question`), sources and referencing (`ee-sources`), structure and argument (`ee-structure`), reflection (`ee-reflection`)
- One course per subject, seeded from that subject's guide (see `ib-biology.md` for an example).

## Skills the programme rewards

Analysis and evaluation rather than recall, clear use of command terms (define, describe, explain, evaluate, discuss, to what extent), interpreting data, and planning long pieces of work over months.

## Common traps

- Leaving IAs and the EE until the last term, then clashing with exam revision.
- Treating command terms loosely: "evaluate" needs a judgment with weighed strengths and limitations.
- TOK: writing about subject content instead of knowledge questions.

## Using the plugin

- One `/study:course` per subject, plus one for TOK and the EE if useful.
- `/study:exam-plan` across all six subjects for the final exam session, with IA and EE deadlines added as fixed tasks.
- Academic integrity is central in the IB: IAs, the EE and TOK work must be your own. Claude can explain concepts, quiz you and give feedback on your plan, but won't write or rewrite assessed work, and you should follow your school's AI-use policy and acknowledge any AI use as it requires.
