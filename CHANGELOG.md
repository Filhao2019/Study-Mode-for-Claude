# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-24

- **Study Mode becomes a full study plugin.** The plugin id changes from `study-mode` to `study` (existing installs are renamed automatically) and the marketplace is now `studying-with-claude`.
- New skills: course organization, ingest (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, images, web, YouTube) with source citations, local Whisper transcription, chat with your materials, rich notes, adaptive quizzes, FSRS flashcards with Anki export, review podcasts, exam plans, weak-spots dashboard, timed mock exams, past-paper analysis, explain-like, Feynman checks, one-page cheat sheets, and an error log with review.
- New agents: `source-checker` and `exam-grader`.
- Self-contained HTML quiz, flashcard, mock-exam and dashboard apps.
- Exam guides for ENEM, SAT, AP Biology, AP Calculus AB, AP U.S. History, AP Chemistry, IB Diploma and IB Biology.
- Study Mode now hands off to the toolkit's skills while keeping its Socratic style.
- Worked photosynthesis example in `examples/photosynthesis/`.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-23

- First release of **Studying with Claude**.
- On/off toggle that persists for the conversation.
- Socratic prompts, hint ladder and self-reflection questions.
- Scaffolded lessons with roadmaps and layered explanations.
- Knowledge checks, personalized feedback and progress recaps.
- Homework and writing-assignment guardrails.
- Support for notes, slides, PDFs, syllabi and photos of problems.
- Study plans, timed reviews, quizzes and flashcards.
- Pace and level adjustment, memory personalization, visuals and voice.
- Claude Code plugin and marketplace manifests.
